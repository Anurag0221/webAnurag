Who I Am

The Mind Behind the Lens

🎓 BSc Animation · Modern College, Shivajinagar, Pune - 05

I'm Anurag Kamble — known creatively as Ani — a Pune-based visual artist who lives and breathes motion, light, and storytelling. My journey started with a lens and a timeline, and it never really stopped.

I graduated with a BSc in Animation from Modern College of Arts, Science and Commerce, Shivajinagar, Pune-05 — but the real education happened on set, in editing suites, and at 2am when the perfect cut finally clicked.

I'm not just an editor who cuts clips together. I'm a cinematographer who understands light, a photographer who sees poetry in stillness, a motion graphic artist who breathes life into static design, and a graphic designer who crafts visual identities. Every discipline feeds the other.

My philosophy: If it doesn't feel like something — cut it. If it doesn't say something — rethink it. If it doesn't hit — it's not done yet.
